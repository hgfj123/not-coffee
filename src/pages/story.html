Our story

It is easier to say what it isn't.

Tea, brewed, canned cold, and nothing added to make it easier to like.

The house it comes from.

Walk into a Middle Eastern home and before you've sat down, there's tea. Real leaves, steeped properly, poured by someone who has made it ten thousand times. Nobody asks if you want it. Nobody adds a miracle to it.

That's the glass we wanted in a can. So we built our own brew and our own flavors from scratch. No generic concentrate, no sugar, no shortcuts. Then we canned it cold.

Every flavor starts in that kitchen: a pot that's always on, a glass for whoever walks in, and nothing stirred in to cover up the tea.

“In our culture, we share tea with the people we love. Here's to you.”

Why the name

The afternoon ritual, without the coffee.

A cold can for when the day needs a lift. Made from tea.

Why unsweetened

Nothing to hide behind.

Take the sugar out and the tea has to be good.

Why so few claims

Three we can prove, not eight we can't.

No probiotics, no cures, no gimmicks. Just a short label.

Drawn, not stock.

Every botanical on the can is drawn by hand in colored pencil. One for each flavor.
